## Status legend

Chapter 11.6 §2, unchanged: **Not Started** · **In Progress** · **Built (unverified)** · **Verified** (Volume 9 tests passing) · **Shipped** (in a released version, Chapter 11.5).

### Two rules from §4, applied literally

> *"A row moves to 'Verified' only once its Volume 9 test coverage (Chapters 9.6/9.7) actually passes in CI — **not on the developer's local machine**."*

**This rule became satisfiable at Mission 4.3, was made unsatisfiable by Mission 5, and Missions 6 and 7 have satisfied it again.** The pipeline first executed on 2026-08-16 at Mission 4.3.7 and reported 10 of 10 at run #13 against a 789-test suite — on `67cd79b`, which Mission 5 then left 64 commits behind. **That backlog is cleared.**

Every mission from 6 onward merged through a pull request, and a pull request's checks run on the **merge result** — so the evidence is not merely current, it is current *for the combination*. PR #25's checks tested Mission 7.11 on top of 7.10, 7.9, 7.8, 7.7 and 7.6.

**Thirteen jobs in `ci.yml`, plus the PR-title check.** They include a full `flutter test`, the backend suite, `terraform validate`, and — since Mission 7.10 — an **APK build from a clean checkout**, which no local build can substitute for: A-205 is a defect in exactly that local command, where `flutter build apk` reported success while reading a stale artefact (A-232).

**Measured at this revision: 1141 mobile tests and 251 backend tests, all passing**, plus a backend coverage gate (A-231) that did not exist when this file was last written.

> **The four `⚠ stale` rows are refreshed, not re-annotated.** The condition that stranded them — evidence 64 commits old with no CI run since — no longer holds. Their Volume 9 coverage has passed in CI against the current HEAD, which is exactly what Chapter 11.6 §4 asks for, so the annotation is removed rather than carried forward.

**One row moves on a different kind of evidence, and says so in its own text.** FR-UPL is Verified as of Mission 7.4, and CI is only part of why. Chapter 11.6 §4's bar is about CI; a real upload to a real endpoint is a claim CI structurally cannot make, and the row distinguishes the two rather than letting the stronger word cover both.

> *"A row moves to 'Shipped' only once Chapter 11.5 has a dated changelog entry for it."*

`docs/CHANGELOG.md` now carries Mission 7 in every section. **It did not until Mission 7.12.** Mission 7.11's security review found the file untouched since Mission 7.4's close — six merged pull requests, #19 through #24, including the two most security-relevant changes in Mission 7 — and wrote the three Security entries that rule required; 7.12 wrote the Added, Changed and Fixed entries for 7.5 through 7.11.

Every entry still sits under `[Unreleased]`, and Chapter 11.5 §4 makes the first dated section v1.0.0 at Milestone M13. **No row can be Shipped yet, by construction** — that conclusion is unchanged, only the fact underneath it was stale.

### One caveat that qualifies every Verified row below

Chapter 9.5 §2's data-layer target is **80%+**. **Re-measured 2026-08-21: 70.6%** (584/827) — *down* from 71.7% at Mission 5, and the reason is worth stating: `isar_chunk_store.dart` grew from 212 lines to **220** while its covered count stayed at **4**. The entire miss is still that one file, at **1.8%** (4/220), a quarter of the layer; excluding it the layer reads **95.6%** (580/607). The whole of `lib/` reads **83.0%** (3369/4059). That file cannot be unit-tested without downloading a native Isar binary at test time, which Mission 4.7 considered and declined (A-096, open item 58), so **device verification is its declared standard**.

Every row below that depends on local storage therefore rests partly on device passes rather than on CI. That is stated here rather than buried, because it is the one place a Verified row means something narrower than it appears.
